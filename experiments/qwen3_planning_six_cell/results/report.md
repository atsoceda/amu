# Published planning features in Qwen3 (a/an and el/la): report

_Sources: `results/<model>/summary.json`, `six_cell_rows.jsonl`, `validation.json`; `results/leverage_analysis.json`;
`results/el_la/...`. Tables generated by `../analyze.py` are in `tables.md`. Design (frozen before results) and
deviations: `../README.md`._

## Motivation

Hanna & Ameisen (2026) find features that represent a future word and cause the
context that licenses it (the article). The reviewers asked for an intervention
selected **without any article criterion**. Their planning features are exactly
that: chosen because they represent the planned word. The question is how their
effect on the planned word travels: through the article the model writes
(**emission**) or with the article fixed (**persistence**).

## What was measured

Their selection rule reproduced for Qwen3-0.6B, 1.7B and 4B, with transcoders
streamed (`skills/hf-range-streaming`). Their interventions (planning features
zeroed, or set to 5x), plus random-feature controls, in the six-cell assay.
Outcome: the planned word's log-probability at \(\tau=1\), split into emission
and persistence.

Selection fidelity (exact per-prompt match of node counts with the authors'
released table): 344 / 338 / 297 of 349 prompts at 0.6B / 1.7B / 4B; totals
within one or two nodes.

## Results: a/an

| Model | 5x: emission / persistence | Zeroed: emission / persistence | Article switches (5x) |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 0.6B | +0.059 / +0.009 | about 0 | 0% |
| 1.7B | +0.045 / -0.002 | -0.010 / -0.004 | at most 0.4% |
| 4B | +0.187 [0.112, 0.269] / +0.012 | -0.173 [-0.230, -0.120] / -0.015 | 9.5% |

Random controls are near zero at every size.

**Leverage (4B).** Per prompt, leverage = log p(planned | right article) - log
p(planned | wrong article). Persistence stays at about ±0.013-0.016 across
leverage tertiles, while emission scales with leverage: emission share is
0.53-0.82 in the lowest-leverage tertile and 0.94-0.96 elsewhere. Policy
movement x leverage predicts emission better than policy movement alone (r =
0.886 vs 0.831).

## Results: el/la

Null at 0.6B and 1.7B, consistent with the authors' published null (their
Appendix D, "the interventions have little effect"). The 4B run was stopped
after 31 prompts as uninformative. At 4B, 71% of selected el/la nodes match only
the English translation, a lead recorded in the README.

## Reading

At 4B, where planning appears, published planning features act on the planned
word almost entirely by emission: they shift the article, and the article
settles the noun. The share of emission is set by leverage, not fixed. The
planning features are decodable and causal, but their downstream effect is not
carried privately.

## Caveats

Qwen3 only; planning is weak below 4B; el/la is uninformative for routes because
the features barely move the article. The el/la candidate set is a documented
superset of the authors' (all active features rather than pruned-graph nodes).

## Place in the thesis

The **leverage law** and **decodable is not carried**, on an independently
selected, published intervention.
