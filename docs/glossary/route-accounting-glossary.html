Route Accounting Glossary

One vocabulary for how an intervention's effect reaches a later token. Each term has a mechanistic meaning, the measurement that stands for it, and the current state of evidence, so the framing and the results can be written without drifting terms.

2. The accounting

Two questions, two axes. Route asks how an effect travels: through which token gets emitted (emission) or through internal states (persistence, split into the paths of section 3). Observability asks whether a reader of the text could recover the information carried (visible vs hidden). The axes are independent: persistence always travels through internal states, yet what it carries can be fully readable from the text. The NeurIPS workshop’s public/private is the route axis only (emission/persistence); it never meant visible/hidden.

The total effect of an intervention on the target, with the mediator generated freely, splits exactly: Etotal = Eemission + Epersistence. Here Y(i,b) is the target distribution with the intervention off (0) or on (1) and mediator b; Bi is the mediator the model emits; qi is the mediator policy.

Emission formerly public route

Meaning
The part of the effect carried by which token gets emitted.
Measure
Replay the induced token with the intervention off: Y(0,B1) − Y(0,B0), and the τ-weighted mixture version.
Status
MethodFound in every setting below

Policy movement

Meaning
How much the intervention changes the mediator's probability.
Measure
q1 − q0
Status
Found

Leverage

Meaning
How much the target depends on which mediator token was emitted, with the intervention off.
Measure
TV(Yb, Yb′), or log p(target | right mediator) − log p(target | wrong mediator).
Status
Found

Emission identity

Meaning
For a binary mediator, emission equals policy movement times leverage.
Measure
TV(Eemission) = |q1 − q0| · TV(Yb, Yb′)
Status
Method exact algebra, not an empirical claim

Persistence formerly private route

Meaning
The effect that remains with the mediator token held fixed. A leftover category, not a mechanism: it splits into the paths below. Private in mechanism (internal states), which says nothing about whether its content is hidden (see Hidden).
Measure
Y(1,B1) − Y(0,B1), and the reverse order.
Status
Found

3. Persistence paths

In a transformer every persistent effect travels through attention, so persistence splits by which positions carry it. Each path is removed by giving chosen positions the clean (intervention-off) keys and values.

Direct retrieval formerly anchored retrieval, part of carried state

Meaning
The target position attends straight back to t and reads its altered state.
Measure
Effect removed when the target position gets clean keys/values at t.
Status
Found couplets: 68–95% of persistence across Qwen3 1.7B–32B and Gemma 3 1B–27B, chat and plain prompts, instruct and base checkpoints; also under the authors’ feature steering (4B–14B); blocking it breaks rhyming up to 14B (authors’ data). For a/an, persistence is this path by construction.

Relay formerly carried private plan

Meaning
Positions between t and the target read t and carry the change forward in their own states. Two kinds matter: generated-text relay (through positions the model wrote) and boundary storage (through a structural prompt position, then read back by retrieval).
Measure
Effect removed when the in-between positions get clean keys/values at t (sufficiency and necessity versions, section 5).
Status
Found small in couplets but rising with scale: median share 4 / 8 / 8 / 11 / 13% at Qwen3 1.7B–32B (+6.2 pp per decade, 95% CI 2.8–9.6). The growth is boundary storage, not generated-text relay. The relay-heavy tail is line-1 copies and ratio noise. Derived values are not relayed (see Recomputation).

Re-reading formerly text-reading, route (a)

Meaning
For text edits, later positions read the changed visible words.
Measure
Effect removed when the changed words' keys/values are made clean.
Status
Hinted A/B text-edit persistence of 0.9–1.3; path not isolated yet

Generated-text relay

Meaning
Relay through positions the model generated (in couplets, line-2 words more than 3 tokens before the rhyme word).
Measure
Only those positions given their edited-run states (sufficiency), or reset to clean (necessity).
Status
Found about 0% at every size and in both families (−0.1 to +0.3 nats). Line-2 relay sits in the last 3 positions: the late lookup of the authors’ circuit, read in two hops.

Boundary storage

Meaning
The plan is also written to the token that ends the line and read back from there later: relay through one structural prompt position, then retrieval.
Measure
Per-token sufficiency and necessity over the positions between line 1 and line 2.
Status
Found appears between 8B and 14B in Qwen3 (both prompts) and by 27B in Gemma 3 (12B in the plain prompt); necessary for about 11% of persistence at Qwen3-32B and Gemma 3 27B; carried by the comma ending line 1 in Qwen3-14B, Qwen3-32B and Gemma 3 27B (chat-template tokens carry nothing); present in base checkpoints. Consistent with line-end planning sites Published (Lindsey et al. 2025; Ma & Rui 2026).

Recomputation

Meaning
A derived value (a sum, a variable chain) is computed again at the answer from the visible source tokens instead of being carried from where it was first computable.
Measure
Donor states at every position after the sources change nothing; donor states at the source digits flip the answer.
Status
Found two-digit sums at Qwen3 1.7B–32B; variable chains at 1.7B–8B (block edit about 0 vs +18 to +47 at the source digits); chain accuracy collapses with length (8B: 100 / 63 / 17% at 1 / 3 / 5 steps).

Hidden

Meaning
An observability property, not a route: information carried by any persistence path whose content a monitor reading the text and tokens could not recover. Persistence is not hidden by default: in every task so far, what it carries (the rhyme sound, the operands) is readable from the text.
Status
Method untested so far (no task yet carries unrecoverable content). Hidden influence on outputs is Published behaviourally (unverbalized hints in chain of thought; filler-token computation and encoded reasoning after special training), without a route-level account. A hidden-choice experiment is the Hypothesis test.

4. Regularities

Leverage law

Claim
Emission share scales with policy movement × leverage; persistence stays roughly constant.
Evidence
a/an, Qwen3-4B: emission share 0.53–0.82 in the lowest-leverage third vs 0.94–0.96 in the other two; persistence about ±0.013–0.016 across leverage; the product predicts emission better than policy movement alone (r = 0.886 vs 0.831). A/B, Qwen3-4B: emission 0.559 at leverage 0.56 vs about 0 at leverage about 0; 10/10 units, p = 0.001.
Status
Found at 4B only; effects at 0.6B and 1.7B are too small to test it

Dialable leverage

Claim
A mediator's leverage can be set by how reliably the context ties it to the target.
Evidence
A/B code at 4B: leverage 0.64 / 0.37 / −0.20 at 100 / 75 / 50% reliability.
Status
Found behavioural, 5 families

Leverage reflects association strength

Claim
Leverage is higher for more entrenched token associations (pretraining agreement above an in-context code) and grows with scale.
Evidence
a/an median leverage 3.3–5.0 nats. A/B code leverage 0 / 0.24 / 0.64 at 0.6B / 1.7B / 4B.
Status
Hinted two association types, three sizes; the n-gram-frequency link is a Hypothesis

Lost decision

Claim
A decision held only in state, with no leveraged mediator, does not reach the target by another path.
Evidence
A/B state patch in the zero-leverage bank: total effect +0.004.
Status
Found one setting, 5 families

Anchor weighting

Claim
Retrieval favours structural anchors: information placed at the end of line 1 is retrieved far more than the same information placed elsewhere.
Evidence
Couplets: donor rhyme state at the anchor vs other line-1 positions gives persistence +12.1 vs +3.5 to +6.0 (1.7B) and +22.6 vs +5.1 to +10.7 (8B); relay small from every position.
Status
Found weighted, not exclusive: the anchor-only prediction was partly supported

Decodable ≠ carried

Claim
A feature can represent a future word and cause it, yet its downstream effect travels by emission.
Evidence
Published a/an planning features at 4B: about 92–94% emission, leverage-dependent. Our reproduction of their selection matches exactly on 344 / 338 / 297 of 349 prompts at 0.6B / 1.7B / 4B.
Status
Found planning features themselves Published (Hanna & Ameisen)

5. Validity concepts

Mediator support

Meaning
The decomposition is valid only if the mediator tokens keep most of the probability mass.
Evidence
Failures: bare neutral template (a/an mass about 0.27); 1B effect-matched coalition (called dominates); A/B 50% bank at 4B (0.864).
Status
Found used as a gate

Readout component

Meaning
The part of a steering vector along the mediator's local readout direction, g = ∂(logitb′ − logitb) / ∂h.
Evidence
About 4% of the named-donor vectors' length.
Status
Found

Construction artifact

Meaning
A route effect produced by how the vector was built, which injects the mediator decision, rather than by the model.
Evidence
Removing the readout component: matched-triad interaction 0.120 → 0.018; independent-family interaction 0.264 → 0.057, cross-class emission dominance 6/6 → 1/6 families; target efficacy unchanged.
Status
Found generality to common steering vectors is a Hypothesis

Sufficiency and necessity

Meaning
Two ways to credit a group of in-between positions. Sufficiency: only that group gets its edited-run state, the source stays clean. Necessity: the source is edited and only that group is reset to clean. They agree when paths add; when they interact, necessity exceeds sufficiency. The Shapley average of the two is an interaction-robust summary.
Status
Method

Additivity gap

Meaning
Persistence minus (direct retrieval + relay): how far the paths fail to add. A large positive gap signals redundant storage that the target combines.
Status
Found within about ±5% of persistence in most models; +29% in Gemma 3 27B (chat), where necessity reveals the boundary storage that sufficiency understates.

Positive control (induction)

Meaning
A task where relay is the known mechanism: in … A B … A → B, the first A reaches the prediction through B’s position (previous-token and induction heads).
Status
Found the accounting assigns about 0–2% to direct retrieval and the effect to relay at the key position in Qwen3 1.7B–14B and Gemma 3 12B/27B; partial at Qwen3-32B (direct retrieval 31%). The method can detect relay, so the relay nulls elsewhere are informative.

Mediator-relative routing also lossy public projection

Claim
The article's ability to distinguish targets determines the route.
Evidence
The effect came from the construction artifact.
Status
Retracted

7. The larger picture

Thesis (current evidence). Future-relevant information crosses positions by emission, scaled by leverage, or by retrieval: a later position reading the source position, a structural anchor that stores it, or the visible sources (recomputation). Generated-text relay is about zero in every task measured up to 32B, although the method detects relay where it exists (induction). With scale, plans gain boundary storage at the line-ending token. Whether any path carries hidden content is untested.
